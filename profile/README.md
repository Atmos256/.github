## About Atmos256

Atmos256 is a self-funded atmospheric research and engineering initiative based in North Alabama, USA.

Founded in 2026, Atmos256 explores the intersection of **meteorology, environmental instrumentation, embedded systems, software engineering, and field-based atmospheric observation**.

Our mission is to develop accessible environmental monitoring technologies, experiment with remote data acquisition systems, contribute to open-source development, and document atmospheric phenomena through hands-on research and storm observation.

Atmos256 is built on a straightforward principle:

**Meaningful scientific exploration begins with curiosity, experimentation, and a willingness to share what we learn.**

## Areas of Exploration

### Atmospheric Science & Field Observation
- Severe weather observation and storm documentation
- Meteorological analysis and atmospheric data interpretation
- Environmental monitoring and local weather measurements
- Educational weather photography and documentary production

### Embedded Systems & Environmental Sensors
- ESP32 and Arduino-based sensor development
- Temperature, humidity, pressure, and environmental instrumentation
- Sensor calibration, accuracy testing, and data validation
- Low-power electronics and remote monitoring systems

### Telemetry & Network Engineering
- IoT communication protocols and networking
- Mobile and cellular data gateways
- Edge computing and remote data acquisition
- Home laboratory infrastructure and telemetry endpoints

### Software & Open-Source Development
- Python, C/C++, Java, and Kotlin
- Weather APIs, data processing, and visualization
- Android-based field applications
- Open-source collaboration and technical documentation

## Current Development Focus

Atmos256 is currently in its foundational development stage.

Initial projects focus on building the knowledge, infrastructure, and software necessary to support future remote environmental monitoring systems.

| Initiative | Focus | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Atmos256 Infrastructure | Home laboratory and development environment | In Progress |
| Weather Data Acquisition | Public weather APIs and local data storage | Planned |
| AtmosNode | Experimental microcontroller-based environmental sensor platform | Planned |
| Field Telemetry | Mobile gateway and remote data transmission | Long-Term Goal |
| Field Observations | Atmospheric photography, storm documentation, and reports | Developing |

Project repositories and technical documentation will be published as development progresses.

## Long-Term Vision

Our long-term objective is to design and deploy independent environmental monitoring systems capable of collecting atmospheric measurements in the field and transmitting those observations to a centralized data infrastructure.

The envisioned system will connect:

**Environmental Sensors → Edge Devices → Mobile/Cellular Gateway → Home Lab Endpoint → Data Storage & Visualization**

Each stage will be developed through incremental experimentation, testing, and documentation.

Beyond engineering, Atmos256 aims to make atmospheric science approachable through educational content, storm documentation, and practical open-source projects.
